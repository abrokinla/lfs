Welcome to Life Faith School

A Missionary School With a Heart for Every Child

Life Faith School is a modern missionary school based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State. Our aim is simple but powerful — to provide low-cost, quality education to everybody, including the less privileged.

From Nursery through Primary to Senior Secondary 2, we nurture confident, morally upright and academically excellent young people prepared to change the world.
