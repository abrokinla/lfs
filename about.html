About Life Faith School

A modern missionary school with a mission to make quality education accessible to every child.

Our Story

Raising The Next Generation Through Faith & Knowledge

Life Faith School was founded out of a deep conviction that every child in Rivers State — regardless of family background — deserves access to quality, affordable education.

As a modern missionary school, we blend strong academics with sound Christian values. Our classrooms are filled with curious young minds from Nursery through Primary, Junior Secondary and Senior Secondary 2, each one supported by dedicated teachers and a caring, disciplined environment.

We believe education is the greatest equaliser. That is why our fees are kept deliberately low — so that no child, including the less privileged, is left behind.

Our Mission

To provide low-cost, quality education to every child within our reach — including the less privileged — in a God-fearing, academically excellent and morally sound environment.

Our Vision

To raise confident, empathetic and purpose-driven young people who excel academically, serve their communities faithfully, and transform our nation.

Core Value: Excellence

We pursue the highest academic standards in everything we do.

Core Value: Faith

Christian principles guide our conduct, curriculum and community.

Core Value: Inclusion

Every child, including the less privileged, has a place here.

Core Value: Service

We nurture children who serve God and their communities.
