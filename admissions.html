What You Need

Entry Requirements

Requirements for each level are simple and straightforward.

Age Range

Children aged 2 – 5 years, potty-trained and ready for structured play.

Documents

Birth certificate and 4 passport photographs.

Medical

Health/immunisation record and completed medical form.

Interview

A friendly play-based assessment with our nursery teacher.

Age Range

Children aged 5 – 11 years entering Basic 1 – 6.

Documents

Birth certificate, previous school report card (Basic 2+), and 4 passport photographs.

Medical

Health/immunisation record and completed medical form.

Assessment

Simple written and oral assessment in English and Mathematics.

Age Range

Students aged 11 – 14 years entering JSS 1 – 3.

Documents

Birth certificate, previous term report card, transfer certificate and 4 passport photographs.

Medical

Completed medical form with health/immunisation records.

Entrance Exam

Entrance examination in English, Mathematics and General Knowledge.

Age Range

Students aged 15+ years entering SSS 1 – 2.

Documents

Birth certificate, JSS report cards, transfer certificate and 4 passport photographs.

Medical

Completed medical form with health records.

Subject Selection

Choice of Science, Commercial or Arts stream with career guidance.
