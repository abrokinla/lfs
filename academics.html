Academics & Curriculum

A complete academic journey — from the first steps in Nursery to confident Senior Secondary students.

Choose a Level

Our Academic Levels

Select a level to explore the curriculum, subjects and learning environment for each stage.

Nursery School

Ages 2 – 5

Our nursery section provides a warm, playful and God-centred environment where our youngest learners begin their journey. Through structured play, songs, storytelling and hands-on activities, children develop early literacy, numeracy and social skills.

Early Literacy

Phonics, letter recognition and pre-reading skills through rhymes and story time.

Early Numeracy

Counting, shapes, patterns and basic number sense through fun activities.

Social & Moral Development

Sharing, kindness, prayer and Bible stories that shape good character.

Creative Arts & Play

Drawing, singing, dancing and free play that spark curiosity and imagination.

Primary School

Ages 5 – 11 (Basic 1 – 6)

Primary education at Life Faith School builds strong foundations in literacy, numeracy and scientific thinking. Our teachers use modern, child-centred methods to make learning engaging, while daily devotion and moral instruction keep values at the core.

English Language

Reading, writing, grammar and oral communication for confident expression.

Mathematics

Number work, geometry, problem-solving and mental arithmetic.

Basic Science & Tech

Exploring nature, simple experiments and early introduction to technology.

Social Studies & Civic

Understanding our culture, community and responsibilities as citizens.

Christian Religious Knowledge

Bible knowledge, morals and values that guide everyday living.

Physical & Health Education

Sports, games and healthy living habits for strong bodies and minds.

Junior Secondary School

JSS 1 – 3

Junior secondary education introduces students to a broad and balanced curriculum in line with the Nigerian Basic Education framework. Students develop critical thinking, deepen their knowledge across subjects, and begin discovering their strengths and interests.

English Studies

Advanced reading, composition, literature and public speaking.

Mathematics

Algebra, geometry, statistics and logical problem-solving.

Basic Science

Physics, chemistry and biology foundations through experiments.

Business Studies

Introduction to commerce, bookkeeping and entrepreneurship.

Computer Studies

Digital literacy, typing, word processing and safe internet use.

Civic Education

Citizenship, national values and leadership development.

Senior Secondary School

SSS 1 – 2

Our senior secondary students specialise in Science, Commercial or Arts streams, gaining the depth and discipline needed to excel at WASSCE, NECO and beyond. With mentoring, career guidance and moral instruction, we prepare young people for university and life.

Science Stream

Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Further Maths and Agricultural Science.

Commercial Stream

Accounting, Commerce, Economics, Financial Accounting and Marketing.

Arts Stream

Literature-in-English, Government, CRS, History and Fine Arts.

Examination Readiness

Targeted coaching and mock exams for WASSCE, NECO and JAMB preparation.
